01 How to Play
Simple rules. Serious stakes.
💰

1. Pick Your Stake

Choose an entry fee from 0.1 WLD up to 50 WLD — 11 tiers. Higher stake = bigger pot.

🔍

2. Get Matched

Paired 1v1 with a real player at the same fee tier. Payment must be verified on-chain first — ghost/abandoned matches are never joined.

🎲

3. Roll 15 Times

Each player gets 15 rolls in a 32-second round (30s gameplay + 2s result). One roll every 2 seconds — the die never lies (server-validated).

🏆

4. Highest Score Wins

When the timer hits zero, the higher total score takes the pot. Winner payout + TNV is paid instantly, automatically.

04 Security Features
Built to be cheat-proof and drain-proof. The server never trusts the client.
🔗

On-Chain Payment Verification

"Payment success" is never trusted alone. Every payment is verified against the real WLD transfer — and the transfer must postdate the match (no reused/stale payments).

🎲

Server-Validated Dice Rolls

Rolls are validated server-side (value 1–6, 15-turn cap, participant-only, turn-lock). Hacking the client can't change your score — the database is the source of truth.

🛡️

Participant & Payment Checks

Refunds and payouts are only issued to a paid participant of that exact match — verified server-side. Forged requests are rejected.

🔒

RLS-Protected Database

Row Level Security blocks direct writes with the public key. Every state change goes through validated SECURITY DEFINER functions — nobody can edit scores, balances or match state directly.

💸

Automatic Refund System

Cancel, or no opponent in 60 seconds? A refund is queued and executed by a cron-driven resolver within one minute. Real payments always come back.

🚫

No Double Payouts

Settlement is claimed atomically (one-time flags) — duplicate settle calls can never pay the pot twice, and duplicate tx-hashes are globally rejected (1 payment = 1 match).

👻

Ghost Match Cleanup

Abandoned matches (app closed mid-flow) are auto-cancelled and auto-refunded. No ghost waiting matches, no joining dead games.

🧾

Admin-Gated Operations

Withdrawals and blocks are approved only by the verified admin wallet — every admin function rejects strangers.

⛔

Anti-Drain Protection

Unpaid strangers can't queue refunds. Cancelled-before-payment matches vanish entirely — no fake refunds, no wallet draining.

08 Payment & Refund Flow
What happens behind the scenes, step by step.

🎫 You join the arena

A match is created with your chosen fee tier — status waiting.

💳 You pay the entry fee

WLD is sent via World App to the escrow contract. The payment is verified on-chain — only a real, fresh payment flips your flag to paid.

🤝 Opponent joins

When a second player at the same fee pays (both verified on-chain), the match connects and both flags are paid. One payment can never book two matches.

🎲 Battle begins

15 rolls each, 32-second round. Scores are recorded server-side on every roll, in realtime on both screens.

🏆 Winner settled

The higher score wins. The winner's exact payout + TNV is sent instantly from the escrow — validated and one-time only. Loser gets TNV consolation.

↩️ Refund (if no opponent)

Cancel anytime, or if no opponent arrives in 60s, your WLD is automatically refunded within a minute via the refund resolver. Support bot can verify it with your tx-hash anytime.
