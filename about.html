01 How to Play
Simple rules. Serious stakes.
💰

1. Pick Your Stake

Choose an entry fee from 0.1 WLD up to 50 WLD. Higher stake = bigger pot.

🔍

2. Get Matched

You're paired with a real player at the same fee tier. Matchmaking only pairs you with fresh, active matches — ghost/abandoned matches are never joined.

🎲

3. Roll 15 Times

Each player gets 15 rolls in a 60-second round. One roll every 2 seconds — the die never lies (server-validated).

🏆

4. Highest Score Wins

When the timer hits zero, the higher total score takes the pot. Winner payout is paid instantly, automatically.

03 Security Features
Built to be cheat-proof and drain-proof. The server never trusts the client.
🔗

On-Chain Payment Verification

MiniKit's "success" is never trusted alone. Every payment is verified on-chain via the actual WLD transfer — and the transfer must postdate the match (no reused/stale payments).

🎲

Server-Validated Dice Rolls

Rolls are validated server-side (1–6, 15-turn cap, participant-only). Hacking the client can't change your score — the database is the source of truth.

🛡️

Participant & Payment Checks

Refunds and payouts are only issued to a paid participant of that exact match — verified server-side. Forged requests are rejected.

🔒

RLS-Protected Database

Row Level Security blocks direct writes with the public key. Every state change goes through validated SECURITY DEFINER RPCs — nobody can edit scores, balances or match state directly.

💸

Automatic Refund System

Cancel or time out (no opponent in 60s)? A refund is queued and executed by a cron-driven resolver within one minute. Real payments always come back.

🚫

No Double Payouts

Settlement is claimed atomically (one-time flag) — a duplicate settle call can never pay the pot twice.

👻

Ghost Match Cleanup

Abandoned matches (app closed mid-flow) are auto-cancelled and auto-refunded. No ghost waiting matches, no joining dead games.

🧾

Admin-Gated Operations

Withdrawals are approved only by the admin wallet (server-verified). Blocking users and approving payouts can't be spoofed from the client.

04 Payment & Refund Flow
What happens behind the scenes, step by step.

🎫 You join the arena

A match is created with your chosen fee tier — status waiting.

💳 You pay the entry fee

WLD is sent via World App to the escrow contract. The payment is verified on-chain before anything else happens.

🤝 Opponent joins

When a second player at the same fee pays, the match is matched and both flags flip to paid.

🎲 Battle begins

15 rolls each, 60-second round. Scores are recorded server-side on every roll.

🏆 Winner settled

The higher score wins. The winner's payout is sent instantly from the escrow — validated and one-time only.

↩️ Refund (if no opponent)

Cancel anytime, or if no opponent arrives in 60s, your WLD is automatically refunded within a minute via the refund resolver.
